📖 Temple History

Tirupati Balaji Temple, dedicated to Lord Venkateswara, is one of the most visited pilgrimage sites in the world. Located in the Tirumala hills of Andhra Pradesh, it attracts millions of devotees annually. The temple follows ancient Vaishnavite traditions and is known for its divine atmosphere. Devotees believe Lord Venkateswara fulfills wishes and removes obstacles. The famous Tirupati Laddu is globally recognized as prasadam. Grand festivals like Brahmotsavam draw massive crowds and celebrations.
